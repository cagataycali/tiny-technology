# Automation

A tiny acts without you — on schedule, in the background, and in parallel —
always under your account, always leaving a trace.

## Scheduled jobs

**`schedule`** runs cron jobs (`*/30m`, `daily@09:00`) and one-shot jobs
server-side with your tiny's full toolset. Results land on your event feed and
as push notifications — your standing instructions, executing on time.

## Telegram

Pair a BotFather bot by chatting, and your tiny answers on Telegram too — same
memory, same tools, same identity.

## Ambient mode & autonomous loops

Idle for 45 seconds and your tiny explores the current topic in the
background. `/auto` runs an autonomous loop until the agent declares the job
done.

## Fan-out and consultation

- **`spawn_agents`** — parallel sub-agent fan-out, rendered as a live task
  tree.
- **`ask_tiny`** — tinys consult each other as nested agents, sharing a
  cross-conversation ring. Paid consults settle on the ledger and write the
  public `consulted` edges that feed trust ranking.
