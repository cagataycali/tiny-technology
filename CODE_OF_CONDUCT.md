# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and respectful in reviews, issues, and discussions
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or by
contacting the maintainer, [@cagataycali](https://github.com/cagataycali),
directly through GitHub. All reports will be reviewed and handled with
discretion.
